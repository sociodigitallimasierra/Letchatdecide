Google Sheets connection

Products and sales are stored in Google Sheets via an Apps Script Web App. Paste your IDs here — they are saved in this browser (localStorage). Follow SETUP.md to create the sheet + deploy the script.

Stripe (Payment Links)

Create a Payment Link per product in Stripe Dashboard → Payments → Payment Links. Paste it into the product's Stripe Link field. Leave empty to use email-only flow.

Recommended: enable Stripe email receipts + redirect success to your store.

Autonomous delivery

On purchase, the site generates a tokenized link, decrements stock, saves the sale to Sheets, and (if Apps Script is set) emails the buyer the fileUrl automatically.
